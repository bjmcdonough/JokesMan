Absolutely Free and Zero Annoyance:  Enjoy our app without spending a dime or enduring pesky ads. It's like finding a unicorn in the app world!


Favorites Feature:  Hoard Your Humor:  Stash away those knee-slappers in your Favorites. It's like a secret joke vault on your device, for those times you need a quick giggle.



History Logging:  A Comedy Chronicle:  Every chuckle you've had is neatly logged in History. It's like having a diary, but funnier and less teenage angst.


JokesMan's Cheeky Grin:  Ever-Present Mirth:  Our JokesMan, with his mischievous smile, photobombs almost every background. It's like having a constant companion in your comedy journey.


JokesMan On The Move, means Animated Amusement:  Watch the JokesMan dance across your screen, adding a sprinkle of whimsy as you navigate through a never-ending joke fest.


Built-In Laugh Track which Amplifies the Fun:  Each joke comes with its own laugh track, because let's face it, everything's funnier with canned laughter.


Joke Sharing Extravaganza:  Share the fun via Email, Text, or even Local Postal Service (because who doesn't love snail mail humor?). Share each joke while viewing via Email, Text and Local Postal Service.


Privacy First means No Creepy Tracking:  We respect your privacy. No location tracking or personal data snooping. All app data is like a hermit - it stays on your device and minds its own business.
